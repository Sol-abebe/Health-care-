National Health Policy of Ethiopia (April 2024)

Anchored on Articles 89 & 90 of FDRE Constitution ensuring equitable health access for all citizens.

National Health Vision
"ጤናማ፣ ምርታማና የበለጸገ ኀብረተሰብ"
"Healthy, Productive, and Prosperous Society"

1. Constitutional Foundation & 7 Guiding Principles

Grounded in FDRE Constitution Articles 89 (Social Objectives) & 90 (Public Health Services).

1. Equity & Inclusiveness (ፍትሃዊነትና አሳታፊነት) Ensuring zero barrier to health based on region, gender, socio-economic status.
2. Quality & Excellence (ጥራትና ድንቅነት) Patient-centered standard care across all healthcare delivery levels.
3. Sustainability (ዘላቂነት) Domestic resource mobilization and resilient system building.
4. Evidence-Based Decision (በመረጃ የተደገፈ ውሳኔ) Data-driven policy formulation and operational research.
5. Transparency & Accountability (ግልጽነትና ተጠያቂነት) Ethical governance at all health administration tiers.
6. Multisectoral Collaboration (የብዙ ዘርፍ ትብብር) One Health approach and health-in-all-policies strategy.
7. Ethical & Culturally Sensitive Service (ሥነ-ምግባራዊና ባህላዊ አከባበር) Respecting indigenous cultural values while upholding international bioethics standard.

2. Ten (10) Key Policy Directions (April 2024)

1. Quality & Equitable Health Services (የጥራትና ፍትሃዊ የጤና አገልግሎት)

Scaling primary health care (PHC) essential package, high-impact maternal, child, and infectious disease interventions.

2. Community Ownership & Empowerment (የህብረተሰብ ባለቤትነት)

Revitalizing Health Extension Program (HEP) towards 2nd Generation standards and urban health models.

3. Emergency Preparedness & Resilience (የድንገተኛ አደጋ ዝግጁነትና ጥንካሬ)

Strengthening Ethiopian Public Health Institute (EPHI), real-time disease surveillance and climate resilience.

4. Hygiene, Sanitation & Environmental Health (ጽዳት፣ አከባቢ ጤና)

Promoting WASH in health facilities, climate-smart health centers, and occupational safety.

5. Food Safety & Nutrition Security (የምግብ ደህንነትና ሥነ-ምግብ)

Eradicating stunting/wasting through multisectoral Seqota Declaration initiatives.

6. Human Resources for Health (HRH) (የጤና ሰው ሀብት)

Building Motivated, Competent, and Compassionate (MCC) professionals and fair retention schemes.

7. Digital Health, Research & Innovation (ዲጂታል ጤናና ምርምር)

Implementing e-Health, electronic Community Health Information System (eCHIS), and localized clinical trial capabilities.

8. Regulatory Capacity & Safety (የጤና ቁጥጥርና ደህንነት)

EFDA regulation of pharmaceuticals, medical equipment, and healthcare facility licensing.

9. Health Financing & Insurance (የጤና ፋይናንስና ኢንሹራንስ)

Expanding Community Based Health Insurance (CBHI) & Social Health Insurance (SHI) for financial protection.

10. Indigenous Knowledge & Traditional Medicine (ባህላዊ ህክምና)

Integrating evidence-based traditional health practices into formal regulatory and research frameworks.

5-YEAR STRATEGIC PLAN

Health Sector Transformation Plan II (HSTP-II)

Execution framework spanning EFY 2013-2017 prioritizing UHC and Woreda Transformation.

Total Costing Base Case
$21.88 Billion USD
High Case: $27.55 Billion USD

5 Core Transformation Agendas

01

Quality & Equity

Ensuring standardized, respectful, and equal care for all population segments.

02

Information Revolution

Digitization of health metrics, eCHIS deployment, and data use culture.

03

MCC Workforce

Motivated, Competent, and Compassionate healthcare professionals.

04

Health Financing

Scaling CBHI coverage above 80% and reducing out-of-pocket expenditure.

05

Leadership

Transforming institutional leadership, governance, and decentralization.

4 Core HSTP-II Strategic Objectives

1. Accelerate Progress Towards Universal Health Coverage (UHC) Expanding reproductive, maternal, newborn, child, adolescent health, and nutrition services.
2. Protect People from Health Emergencies Early warning system, epidemic outbreak control, and public health disaster management.
3. Woreda Transformation Converting districts into high-performing, self-sustaining health administrative units.
4. Enhance Health System Responsiveness Improving patient satisfaction, hospital amenities, and ethical grievance handling.
